# 🏬 Store Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes store sales performance across time, product categories, sales channels, customer gender and shipping regions.

## 📌 Objective
Help business users quickly understand revenue trends, which categories and channels perform best, and where orders are coming from.

## 📊 What's in the Dashboard
- **KPIs:** Total Revenue, Total Orders
- **Trend analysis:** Sales amount over time (line chart)
- **Category analysis:** Sales by product category (bar chart)
- **Channel analysis:** Sales by channel (column chart)
- **Customer analysis:** Sales split by gender (donut chart)
- **Regional analysis:** Sales by ship-state (pie chart)
- **Filters:** Status, Gender, Channel, Category slicers

## 🛠️ Tools
Power BI Desktop, DAX, Power Query

## 💡 Key Insights
- *(Best-selling category and its share of revenue)*
- *(Top channel and top state)*
- *(Gender split)*

## ▶️ How to Use
1. Download `Store_Analysis_Dashboard.pbix`
2. Open it in Power BI Desktop (free)
3. Use the slicers to filter and explore
